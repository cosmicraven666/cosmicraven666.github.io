01 / About Hermes

One assistant for the owner’s work.

Hermes V helps its owner work with email, schedules, and files through requested tasks. It is a personal-use application, not a service open to the public. Account access starts only after the owner grants Google OAuth consent.

Gmail · Calendar · Drive · Docs · Sheets

Access follows the task.

With the owner’s explicit Google OAuth consent, Hermes can work with Gmail messages, Calendar events, and Drive files, including Docs and Sheets, within the permissions granted. It uses that access to carry out the owner’s requests.
